Introduction

Why I chose Digitech and why you should too!

Digitech is one of the most engaging and beneficial subjects you can choose here at Burnside High School. Digitech opens many doors of opportunities for creativity and technological skill to thrive. Digtech helps you discover new interests and teaches you valuable skills. In this subject, we get to learn about many things like coding, html, python, website design, computer science, 3D modelling, animation, design for manufacturing, audio visual, game design and student lead project. Learning these skills are not only useful for school, but also for the future. This subject is especially important nowadays because everything is increasingly dependent on digital technology.
